What it demonstrates

A repeatable alert-triage workflow

The project accepts fictional, vendor-neutral OT alerts and organizes the information an analyst needs before escalating an issue. It was created with AI coding assistance as a portfolio exercise and is not connected to Rahul's employer environment.

  1. Validate the alert.Check the asset, event type, business context, indicators, and required fields.
  2. Assess the risk.Apply visible rules for criticality, exposure, safety impact, and supporting evidence.
  3. Prepare the response.Organize recommended next steps, escalation ownership, and an email draft.
  4. Require analyst approval.The project never performs remediation or changes an OT asset.
